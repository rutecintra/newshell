## what-happened

This page is a sample. In it, the government of a fictional Argentine province introduces a bill about school meals. Here, introduced means the bill has been sent to the legislature. The original report is fictional too, and it is labeled as a sample outlet. The bill has not been voted on.

## in-plain-terms

The mechanism in the sample is simple. The province would pay for one meal on each school day in the public schools named in the bill. A school day is a day when class is held. The chart uses three invented districts and shows, in percent, how many school days would include a meal. A higher value means more days covered, not a score for the quality of the food.

## who-may-be-affected

If a bill like this were real, students in the included schools would be the direct group. Families who now pay for the meal might spend less, and the province would take on a new expense. This sample does not calculate that amount and does not claim that any family has already been affected. The effect depends on the bill being approved and on money being set aside.

## where-things-stand

The stage is introduced. The text has reached the legislature and has not been approved. It is not in force. The next step known in the sample would be discussion in committees. There is no confirmed date for a vote.
